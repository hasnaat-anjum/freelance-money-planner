      Privacy

      There is no account and no server. Everything you enter is stored only in this browser's local storage, on your own device. Clearing your browser data erases it — export a JSON backup from Reports first if it matters to you.

      Danger zone

      Erase every income entry, expense, bill, goal and setting in this app. This cannot be undone.
